# Recorded audio sources

These recordings were published under **Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0)** by their recordists. The game bundles edited, normalized Ogg Vorbis derivatives of the publishers' high-quality Freesound previews. All sound assets are served locally; there are no requests to Freesound during play.

- `desert-wind.ogg`: **desert_wind.wav** by **DarkShroom**, 2022. Source: https://freesound.org/people/DarkShroom/sounds/645305/ . A 24-second excerpt, filtered, normalized and faded for the ambient bed.
- `chitin-crunch.ogg`: **crunch** by **SammygoodTunes**, 2026. Source: https://freesound.org/people/SammygoodTunes/sounds/844811/ . Filtered/normalized; layered and pitch shifted during eating.
- `rock-fall.ogg`: **Falling and rolling stones.wav** by **Tumbleweed3288**. Source: https://freesound.org/people/Tumbleweed3288/sounds/381547/ . A 2.6-second excerpt, filtered, normalized and faded; layered and pitch shifted for emergence, landings and breakage.

License dedication: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Additional pressure tones, grain layers and UI cues are original procedural Web Audio design in `src/game/Audio.ts`.
